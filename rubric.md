\outl0\strokewidth0 \strokec3 The evaluation criteria for assignment 1 in the project, categorising performance into three levels: 
\f1\b \cf2 \strokec3 Insufficient, Sufficient, and Good/Excellent
\f0\b0 \cf2 \strokec3 . It evaluates five key aspects: data inspection, proposal of tests, good and bad model selection, evaluation and reflection, and reproducibility. To achieve a sufficient grade, students must identify at least three data-related problems (including at least one concerning validity), justify their proposed tests using partitioning and metamorphic testing, present and justify both good and bad models, evaluate outcomes using classical metrics and selected tests, and provide reproducible code. To achieve a good or excellent grade, students must go beyond these minimum requirements by demonstrating independent engagement with the data, providing stronger justifications for identified problems and model choices, designing an extensive test suite with justified performance metrics, and presenting a comprehensive reflection on the strengths and weaknesses of the models and the contributions of different subgroups. The code must also be well-documented and well-structured. Overall, the table emphasises not just completing the technical tasks, but demonstrating critical thinking, sound justification, thorough evaluation, and reproducibility.}
